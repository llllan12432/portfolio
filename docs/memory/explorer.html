      How scoring and forgetting work

      After a similarity threshold of 0.35, eligible memories receive a weighted score. The time contribution follows exp(−0.693 × age ÷ half-life): 5 days for short, 21 for medium and 90 for long. Permanent memories are loaded separately.

      “Forgetting” reduces retrieval priority; it does not erase a stored record. The weights are my own design choice, not a proven best setting.
